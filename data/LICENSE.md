## What is here, and where it came from

**`corpus/demo.tsv.gz`** — 200,722 captioned images, sampled from the
[WIT dataset][wit] (Wikipedia-based Image Text), released by Google Research
under CC BY-SA 3.0. Captions and article titles originate with Wikipedia
contributors. Only image URLs are included; no image files are redistributed
here or by WIT.

**`evaluation/strict.tsv`, `evaluation/lenient.tsv`** — 25 passages with
relevance judgements. The passages are Wikipedia article text and so remain
CC BY-SA 3.0. The judgements — which images suit which passage — were produced
for this project and are released under the same licence so the sets can be
used as a whole.

[wit]: https://github.com/google-research-datasets/wit
